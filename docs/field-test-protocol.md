# Tomato cooler-test / shaded-control field protocol

This is a blank protocol for real observations, not evidence that a cooler has worked. Evaporative cooling depends on ambient humidity, airflow, water quality and construction; Buea's ambient humidity may reduce cooling performance. This prototype gives no food-safety certification or universal safe-storage range.

1. Obtain participant consent and choose tomatoes of comparable maturity and initial condition.
2. Divide produce into comparable, clearly labelled cooler-test and shaded-control groups. Record sample size and initial mass (preferably kg), visible damage, variety if known, and harvest date/time.
3. Put the test group in the cooler and control group in ordinary shaded storage. Keep both out of direct sunlight and prevent dirty water or condensation from contacting food. Record materials, placement, airflow, water source, cleaning, and power.
4. Verify thermometer/hygrometer identity, calibration notes, location and clock. Compare with a reference instrument if available.
5. Record temperature °C and relative humidity at consistent times (for example, morning and afternoon). Preserve manual/hardware provenance and do not replace missing readings with estimates.
6. Inspect both groups daily with the same firmness, visible mold, leakage and shriveling checklist. Weigh discarded produce and record remaining saleable mass on the same basis.
7. Record dirty-water/food contact, condensation, cleaning problems, power interruptions, sensor faults, unusual weather and other deviations.
8. Compare readings, observed spoilage and saleable mass only over the same observation period. Report missing observations and trial limitations. Repeat if feasible.

Hygiene: clean food-safe surfaces; keep cooling water separated from produce; prevent drips/condensation; discard contaminated produce according to local guidance. Stop and seek qualified local food-safety guidance if contamination or unsafe handling is suspected.

Use the blank [field-results template](field-results-template.md). No measurements, results, loss reductions or profit changes are supplied here.
